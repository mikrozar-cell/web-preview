The Daycare Vet

The vet that visits your dog!

Stress-free, reliable veterinary care for your canine companion while at doggy daycare so you don’t have to worry about a thing!

The veterinary clinic can often be a stress-inducing place before you’ve even walked through the door. With me, your dog’s health checks and vaccinations will take place in the familiar, relaxed environment of their daycare. I can arrange a free meet & greet when you register and, with regular visits, I will be able to build a trusting, fun relationship with your pup. To find out more about our benefits, click here .

No more worrying about trying to remember to book your dog’s routine health checks, when their vaccinations are due or when you need to pick up flea and worming products. Drop your dog off at daycare as usual and I will give them their health check, vaccinations and parasite treatments when required. I can also clip nails, empty anal glands and treat non-urgent conditions (such as ear infections, skin complaints and chronic lameness) if necessary. I will keep in touch via the Collar app so you can ask questions and access your pet’s reports easily. For a detailed breakdown of our available memberships click here .
